# FIVERR GIG — Lead Generation System

## Gig Title

I will find targeted business leads with emails using AI automation

## Gig Description

Need leads for your business? I'll set up an automated system that:
- Finds businesses matching your criteria (industry + location)
- Extracts business names, websites, emails, phone numbers
- Saves everything to a Google Sheet
- Runs on-demand or on schedule

**Tech:** n8n + Google Maps scraping (no paid APIs)
**Delivery:** 1-2 days
**Support:** 14 days
